    How the engine works

    Each category has a set of weighted cues, such as "did not understand", "interpreter" or "medical words" for communication, and "invoice", "booking" or "wristband" for clerical issues. A complaint's score for each category is the sum of the cues it contains.

    The highest score sets the primary category. A close runner-up is reported as a secondary category, because many complaints have more than one cause: a misunderstood instruction can lead to a medication error. Confidence reflects how clearly the top category leads.

    Everything runs in your browser. Uploaded files are never sent anywhere.

    Upload format: a CSV with one complaint per row. The engine looks for a column of complaint text, and uses date, department, severity, patient language, staff language and an existing category column when it finds them.
