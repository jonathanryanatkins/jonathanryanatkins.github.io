How the projection works

Time trials and races use the Riegel formula, the standard way of scaling a race result to a longer distance. Easy and long runs give a rougher estimate: half marathon pace is taken as about 45 seconds per km faster than your conversational pace. A time trial in the last four weeks takes priority over easy-run estimates. Workouts are logged but left out, because their times mix warm-ups and recoveries.
