How it works

Finding the videos. The engine searches YouTube for videos on your topic published in the time window and region you chose, takes up to 50 of the most viewed, then ranks them by likes plus comments and keeps the top 10.

Reading them. For each of the ten it reads the title, tags, description and up to 100 of the most relevant comments. Titles count most, and highly liked comments count more than ignored ones. It uses YouTube's official API only, so it does not scrape transcripts.

Finding the themes. It looks for phrases that recur across several of the ten videos, groups related phrases (cash ISA, Lifetime ISA, ISA allowance), and favours what viewers discuss in the comments over what creators simply tag. Each theme comes with the evidence behind it and the question viewers are asking most.

Your privacy. Everything runs in your browser. Your key and topic are sent only to YouTube.

Video data comes from the YouTube Data API. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube. Demo results use invented channels and comments.
